02 / How it works

What each side actually does.

Two products, one loop. People keep an agent honest through a calibration game. Businesses post research and read a graded answer.

2C · The app

Using it is calibrating it.

Built for short, one-handed sessions you open between two things you are already doing. Every session moves your Fidelity Score, and your Fidelity Score is what decides your pay.

The blind reveal
  • 1A business posts a question.
  • 2Your agent answers it. You do not see its answer.
  • 3You answer the same question yourself.
  • 4The reveal: here is what your agent said.
  • 5The match updates your Fidelity Score, and your pay.
You cannot game what you cannot see. Answering blind removes the incentive to make your agent look agreeable, so the calibration data stays honest. That is a selling point to businesses, not just a mechanic.

2B · The research console

Ask a question, get a graded answer.

An enterprise workspace where a study goes from a brief to a defensible answer in minutes, and tells you exactly how far to trust it.

A study, end to end
  • 1Brief. Describe your question, audience, and context to Mira, the research copilot. Drop in personas or past studies to ground it.
  • 2Simulate. The Behavior Engine runs it across calibrated agents: one segment solo, or a whole population in a sandbox.
  • 3Read the result. A prediction with a confidence band, the key drivers, a Fidelity Score, and an honest read on how far to trust it.
  • 4Escalate when it matters. Low fidelity or high stakes? Interview the same calibrated people, then re-run with the lift.
Calibrated supply, not a black box. Every answer traces back to real, authorized people whose agents are scored for how well they match them.

03 / Why us

Two advantages, put together for the first time.

One keeps the supply fresh. The other makes the simulation defensible. Neither is about out-engineering anyone.

01

An approachable app that makes calibration a daily habit.

Everyone else captures behavioral data once, in a long interview or a scraped dump, then watches it go stale. Our app is easy enough to open every day, so the signal stays current. That daily loop is what makes the two-sided market run: people keep calibrating, and businesses keep getting answers that reflect right now, not last year.

02

A causal graph behind every prediction.

We do not prompt an agent to act like a 27-year-old in New York. We model the population's decision structure as a graph of weighted causal links drawn from published science, then personalize it to each user.

Master graph. A population model of weighted causal links, built from large-scale data and the literature.
Individual graph. Your measured traits are clamped in, the rest is inferred. The more we measure, the higher your fidelity.
In-graph vs generative. Known outcomes resolve through the graph with confidence. Novel what-ifs are estimated and fed back in.
